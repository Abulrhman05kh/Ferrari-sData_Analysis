# Ferrari Analytics Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard that analyzes Ferrari's financial performance, car deliveries, model sales and Formula 1 results between 2015 and 2025.

//Overview

The dashboard combines business and racing data on a single page, so you can see how revenue, deliveries and customer satisfaction relate to the team's F1 performance. Slicers let you filter the report by F1 performance, model, year and race wins.

//Key Metrics (KPIs)

- **Best F1 season:** 2018
- **Total revenue (2015-2025):** 93.16B EUR
- **Cars delivered (2025):** 13,752
- **Top revenue by region (Europe, 2025):** 2.36B EUR
- **Customer satisfaction:** 92%

//Visuals

| Visual | What it shows |
| Revenue over time | Sum and average of revenue per year (EUR billion) |
| Average selling price by model | Price trend per model against cars delivered in 2025 |
| Monthly deliveries 2025 | Average cars delivered per month |
| Model sales detail 2025 | Treemap of sales and revenue (EUR M) per model |
| Race wins | Count of race wins by number of wins (donut chart) |

**Models covered:** Roma, 296 GTB, Purosangue, SF90 Stradale, 812 Competizione, Daytona SP3, and others / special series.

## Filters

- Ferrari F1 Performance
- Revenue by Model
- Revenue Over Time
- Races Wins

## Tools

- **[Power BI Desktop]**: report and interactive visuals
- **[DAX]**: measures and calculations
- **[Power Query]**: data cleaning and transformation
- **[Excel / CSV]**: data source

## How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Open it with Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers on the left to filter the data.
